# MAX COMBO cue provenance

`max_combo_original.mp3` is an unmodified copy of the short cue supplied by the project owner, who confirmed it is the original sound they wanted for Aurora's MAX COMBO celebration. The game plays it only when a chart ends without misses. The owner requested the in-game level increase included in this release.

This is an excerpt from a DJMAX RESPECT V recording, not an original Aurora sound or a game data file. The surrounding music and full video are not included. Rights to the underlying sound remain with their respective holders.
